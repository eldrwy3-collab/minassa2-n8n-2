ABOUT NETREGENT

Automation intelligence built around the business goal.

netregent helps users move from “What should I connect?” to “What outcome do I need?” It presents the workflow architecture first, so users can understand the sequence, branches, tools and alternatives before implementation.

What we provide

Searchable automation patterns, interactive workflow maps, explanations, platform choices, alternatives and reusable templates across six major automation ecosystems.

Problems we solve

We reduce trial-and-error node building, unclear branching, tool-selection confusion and the time lost rebuilding the same workflow in several platforms.

How it works

A plain-language request is matched against workflow knowledge, then rendered as a structured architecture with steps and implementation directions.

6 Platforms

n8n, Make.com, Zapier, Pipedream, Node-RED and Power Automate.

10 Domains

HR, Sales, Support, E-Commerce, Content, IT, Real Estate, Finance, Healthcare and Education.

Free-first philosophy

The core catalog and deterministic workflow mapping run without a paid AI API or an n8n runtime.
